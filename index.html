Why Banten?

History is not behind glass here.

It stands in palace walls, rises through a mosque tower, rests beside a quiet lake, and meets the sea at an old fortress. Together, these places make the Banten Sultanate story tangible.

01

Memory · Museum

Museum Banten Lama

Start with artifacts and stories that introduce the Banten Sultanate before seeing its landmarks in person.

02

Power · Palace

Keraton Surosowan

Walk through the remains of the 16th-century royal palace at the heart of the Banten Sultanate.

03

Architecture · Royal residence

Keraton Kaibon

See the dramatic arches of a royal residence whose ruins remain among Banten’s most memorable silhouettes.

04

Faith · Landmark

Great Mosque of Banten

Encounter a 16th-century mosque known for its distinctive historic tower and enduring spiritual presence.

05

Landscape · Water

Tasikardi Lake

Pause beside a tranquil historic lake where water, greenery, and the wider landscape soften the trail’s pace.

06

Defense · Colonial chapter

Speelwijk Fortress

Close the journey at a Dutch-built 17th-century fortress that once guarded the coastline.

Leave with respect.

Protect the ruins

Stay on permitted paths and avoid climbing or touching fragile structures.

Respect sacred space
